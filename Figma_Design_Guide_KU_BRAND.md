## OFFICIAL KINGSTON UNIVERSITY VISUAL BRAND

### Color Palette (OFFICIAL KU BRAND COLORS)

#### Primary Brand Colors

**Black (Default - PRIMARY)**
- **For print:** CMYK: C0 M0 Y0 K100
- **For screen:** RGB: R0 G0 B0 / HEX: #1d1d1b
- **Usage:** Default for all text and primary design elements

**BlueBlack (Print only - optional)**
- **For print:** CMYK: C100 M63 Y12 K67
- **For screen (large scale only):** RGB: R0 G43 B73 / HEX: #002b49

#### Secondary Brand Colors (use to highlight, not overwhelm)

**Blue (PMS 2925)**
- **For screen:** RGB: R0 G156 B222 / HEX: #009cde
- **For print:** CMYK: C85 M21 Y0 K0

**Orange (PMS 166)**
- **For screen:** RGB: R227 G82 B5 / HEX: #e35205
- **For print:** CMYK: C0 M76 Y100 K0

**Yellow (PMS 7549)**
- **For screen:** RGB: R255 G181 B0 / HEX: #ffb500
- **For print:** CMYK: C0 M22 Y100 K2

**Green (PMS 368)**
- **For screen:** RGB: R120 G190 B32 / HEX: #78be20
- **For print:** CMYK: C65 M0 Y100 K0

**Middle Blue (PMS 630)**
- **For screen:** RGB: R119 G197 B213 / HEX: #77c5d5
- **For print:** CMYK: C48 M0 Y10 K0

**White**
- RGB: R255 G255 B255 / HEX: #ffffff

#### IMPORTANT BRAND COLOR RULES:
- **Black on white is default** (bold, resolute, high contrast, enhances legibility)
- Secondary colors highlight communications but **should not overwhelm**
- **No faculty-specific colors** - any faculty can use any color from the palette
- Use colors purposefully, not excessively
- Ensure sufficient contrast for legibility

### Typography (OFFICIAL KU STANDARDS)

**Primary Typeface:** **Helvetica Neue**
- Designed for legibility across all sizes
- Modern, neutral, professional
- Cornerstone of Modern Movement design

**Fallback Options (if Helvetica Neue unavailable):** **Arial** or **Inter**

**Size Hierarchy for Presentations:**
- **Slide Titles:** 48-60pt, Helvetica Neue Bold
- **Section Headings:** 32-40pt, Helvetica Neue Bold
- **Body Text:** 24-28pt, Helvetica Neue Regular (must be readable from back of room!)
- **Captions:** 18-20pt, Helvetica Neue Regular

### Logo Usage

**Official Name:** **Kingston University London** (for first use)
- Subsequent references: "Kingston University" or "the University" (capital U)
- **NEVER use "KU"** in external communications

**Logo Format:**
- Square logotype with text ranged left
- **Never recreate or typeset** - use official artwork only
- One color (black default or white reversed)
- Ensure sufficient contrast with background

**Logo Placement on Slides:**
- Bottom corner (subtle, consistent placement)
- PNG format for screen use (72dpi optimized)
- Never scale above 100%

## DESIGN STRATEGY FOR THIS PRESENTATION

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Black and white as primary palette** (authoritative, ambitious, focused on content)
- **Strategic use of secondary colors** for key concepts:
  - **Orange** (#e35205) - Physical making, warmth, craft
  - **Blue** (#009cde) - Digital technology, innovation
  - **Green** (#78be20) - Sustainability (SDG 12), circular economy
  - **Middle Blue** (#77c5d5) - Integration, "phygital" concept

### Visual Principles
- **Clear, modern, professional** with creative flair (reflects School of Art)
- **High contrast** for legibility
- **Authentic student work** over stock photography
- **Diverse representation** in all imagery (inclusive value)
- **Excellence in execution** (ambitious value)

### Layout Grid
- 1920 x 1080px (16:9 standard)
- 60px margins all sides
- 12-column grid for alignment
- Consistent padding and spacing

## ACCESSIBILITY STANDARDS (KU COMMITMENT TO INCLUSIVE)

### Contrast Requirements
- **Body text:** Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- **Large text (24pt+):** Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio
- **Tested combinations:**
  - Black (#1d1d1b) on White (#ffffff) = 20.36:1 ✓ Excellent
  - Blue (#009cde) on White = 3.12:1 ✓ OK for large text only
  - Orange (#e35205) on White = 4.64:1 ✓ Good
  - Green (#78be20) on White = 3.16:1 ✓ OK for large text only
  - Yellow (#ffb500) on White = 1.86:1 ✗ Use for graphics only, not text

### Font Sizing
- **Nothing smaller than 20pt** (readability from distance)
- **Body text 24-28pt minimum** (room viewing)
- **Titles 48pt+** (immediate clarity)

### Color Usage
- **Never rely solely on color** to convey meaning
- Use icons, text labels, or patterns in addition to color
- Ensure colorblind-friendly combinations

### Image Alt Text
- Add descriptions to complex diagrams (for future accessibility)
- Ensure all content is understandable without color alone
